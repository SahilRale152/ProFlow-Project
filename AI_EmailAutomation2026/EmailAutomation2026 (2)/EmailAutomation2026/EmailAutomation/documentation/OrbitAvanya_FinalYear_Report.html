01 · Project Overview

Why this exists

Manually emailing hundreds of CEOs doesn't scale — and gives zero feedback. OrbitAvanya automates the full outreach lifecycle end-to-end: validate → send → track → follow-up → report.

Problem

  • Slow — copy/paste at scale is impossible
  • No feedback — no idea who opened or clicked
  • High risk of mistakes — duplicates, fakes, missed follow-ups
  • No visibility — no bounce detection, no location insight

Solution

  • Reads a bulk lead spreadsheet, validates every email (4 free layers)
  • Sends personalized HTML with open / click / visit / time-on-site tracking
  • Sends in the lead's detected or preferred language
  • Watches the inbox, flags replies & bounces, auto-reminds non-responders
  • Runs hands-free in batches — pauses, checks, resumes automatically
In Scope
  • Automated bulk SMTP sending
  • Multi-layer email validation — zero cost
  • Open / click / visit / time-on-site tracking
  • Reply & bounce detection over IMAP
  • Automatic follow-up reminders
  • Duplicate protection + AUTO-CONTINUE batching
  • Localized / multilingual emails (30+ languages)
  • Excel-based reporting (tracker + report file)
Out of Scope
  • Paid email-deliverability services
  • Full inbound CRM
  • Legal / compliance advisory — leads are pre-scraped; sending at scale must still respect anti-spam law in the target region

What Existed Before

  • A Jupyter notebook that could send basic emails via SMTP
  • A single 1×1 open-tracking pixel (server-side only)
  • Manual per-run operations, no batching or validation

03 · System Architecture

How the pieces fit together

One launcher script drives everything — reading leads, validating, sending, and standing up a local tracker — mirrored in production by a PHP endpoint that survives your laptop going offline.

Path A — Local Flask (dev/fallback)
  • Embedded Flask app on port 6060 — routes /track /click /visit /visit-end /locate /status /events
  • Exposed publicly with cloudflared quick tunnel (*.trycloudflare.com)
  • Named-tunnel option for a fixed domain
  • Updates Excel directly with a thread lock
Path B — PHP on cPanel (production)
  • Server/track.php on public_html/track.php — always-on hosting
  • Recipient actions hit track.php?action=... and are written to tracking_log.json
  • Geolocation via ip-api.com (free)
  • sync_tracking_events.py pulls & applies events to Excel every 60s
Why both? The PHP endpoint survives your laptop being offline — recipients can open emails at any hour. The Flask path gives instant local writes during development. When TRACKING_SCRIPT_URL is set, the PHP path + sync script becomes the production mechanism.

04 · Tracking & Funnel Design

How sends become data

Every email carries a pixel, a click-redirect and a beacon; the funnel below turns a click into a rich Excel signal in seconds.

Data safety: all Excel writes go through threading.Lock, records are flushed in batches of 10, duplicate protection is JSON-backed, and the master Total Sent sheet is append-only — nothing is ever overwritten.
